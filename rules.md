# 📜 Icarus — Server Rules
Welcome to the Icarus clan server for **Glorybound**. By staying here you agree to these rules. The Leader and Officers enforce them, and their decision is final.

## 1. Be respectful
- No harassment, hate speech, slurs, threats or bullying, in chat, voice or DMs.
- Keep arguments out of public channels. If you have a problem with someone, message an Officer.

## 2. Stay safe
- No NSFW, gore or shocking content anywhere, including names, avatars and voice room names.
- Never share personal information, yours or anyone else's: real name, address, school, photos, phone number.
- Never share your Roblox or Discord password or login. Staff will **never** ask for it.
- No scams, fake giveaways or "free Robux" links.

## 3. Follow Discord and Roblox rules
- Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, and Roblox's Terms of Use and Community Standards.
- No exploiting, cheating, or selling accounts or items for real money.

## 4. Use each channel for its purpose
- 💬・general for chat, 🏆・flex to show off drops and wins, 🎯・looking-for-party to find a party.
- No spam, flooding, chain messages or advertising (other servers, channels, links) without an Officer's OK.
- 🍯・honeypot is a trap for spam bots. Posting there gets you **kicked automatically**.

## 5. Looking for a party
- Post with `/lfp`: pick the right mode and say what you need in the notes.
- Only click **Join** if you mean to play, and tell the party if you have to leave.
- So others can join you, set Roblox **Settings → Privacy → Who can join me?** to **Everyone**.
- Chat in 🎯・looking-for-party is cleared after 15 minutes.

## 6. Roles and nicknames
- Run `/verify` (Bloxlink) to link your Roblox account. Your role and nickname then come from your Roblox account and the clan list automatically.
- Your server nickname is your Roblox display name. Don't change it or pretend to be someone else.
- Don't ask staff for clan roles; they follow the in-game clan list.

## 7. Clan membership
- Clan members who haven't been online in Glorybound for more than **1 day** are removed from the clan.
- When you leave the clan list, your Icarus roles and channel access are removed automatically.
- Going to be away? Tell an Officer beforehand.

## 8. Voice
- ➕ Join me to create gives you your own room, which you can rename. Keep the name appropriate.
- No loud noise or soundboard spam, and don't record anyone without their permission.

## 9. Enforcement
- Breaking a rule can lead to a warning, mute, kick or ban, depending on how serious it is.
- Doxxing, scams and NSFW content mean an immediate ban.
- Questions or reports: message an Officer.
